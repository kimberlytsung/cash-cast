# cash-cast
CashCast is a financial forecasting &amp; protection tool built with the Capital One Nessie API, Python, and Streamlit for HackGT. It projects your cash flow, identifies potential overdrafts, and recommends smart actions (like savings transfers or spending adjustments).

# Product Pitch

**Problem**

Most people, espiecally busy university students, are not aware of when they are going to overdraft until it’s too late. Overdraft fees, bounced bills, and stress add up quickly.

**Solution**

CashCast acts like a weather forecast for your bank account:
* Predicts your balances weeks into the future
* Warns you of overdraft risks ahead of time
* Suggests smart actions (like transferring from savings or cutting daily spend)
* Syncs everything directly into your calendar so you can plan life around your money

**Impact**

Reduces overdraft fees, builds financial confidence, and makes budgeting feel interactive and visual.

# Features

**Cash Flow Forecasting**: predict account balances 30–180 days ahead.

**Overdraft Protection:** gives proactive recommendations like transfer from savings and cut discretionary spending

**Stress Test Mode**: Monte Carlo simulations estimate overdraft probability 

**Calendar Export**: sync forecasts and recommendations with Google/Outlook/Apple calendar

**Interactive Dashboard**: tweak daily spend, test scenarios, and download results

# Tech Stack

Backend: Python 3, Capital One Nessie API

Frontend: Streamlit

Data: Pandas, Numpy

Visualization: Matplotlib

Calendar Export: ics.py
